Centime · Search & Demand Generation
Winning Mid-Market AP & AR Search
A data-grounded re-prioritization of Centime's SEO strategy — by lead-gen value, not rank
SEO Strategy Analysis Prepared for: BC June 16, 2026

Centime's current keyword plan is a rank tracker with intent labels — 290 keywords, "P1-CRITICAL" tiers, and copy-pasted rationale, but no search-volume, difficulty, or conversion data. When we ground priorities in three real datasets — Centime's HubSpot pipeline, Semrush organic positions, and a competitor keyword gap — the conclusion is clear and uncomfortable.

Organic search is Centime's best-quality inbound channel (57 customers vs. 3–6 from paid). But ~81% of Centime's organic traffic is finance memes (41%) and branded search (40%) — non-branded, commercial-intent keywords drive roughly 6 visits per month. The money keywords (AP/AR automation) have real demand, but Centime ranks on pages 3–10 while competitors hold positions 1–3. This is a content-depth and authority problem, not a "nudge page 2 to page 1" problem.

The fix: stop over-investing in zero-volume competitor "vs/alternative" terms, double down on the ERP-specific pages that already convert, and build the educational AP/AR content clusters that competitors use to own the category — and that AI answer engines reward.

7. Content & SEO strategy

  1. Make the ERP × Module × Industry page system the flagship. Your best-converting URLs are already this shape — build it out deliberately with real native-integration proof, comparison tables, FAQs, and schema. Avoid thin doorway pages; the moat is genuine integration depth.
  2. Build topical-authority clusters that mirror the winners. Competitors rank for head AP/AR terms via credentialed-author pillars ("What is AP automation") with cited statistics and supporting cluster posts, all internally linked. Replicate the structure for AP, then AR, then cash flow.
  3. Express the "common platform" wedge as a cluster — but keep entry points module-specific. People search "AP automation," not "unified finance platform." Capture them on module/ERP pages, then convert the best-of-breed-on-one-platform story on-page and in a dedicated pillar (functional, buying/contracting, support, and pricing-leverage reasons).
  4. Win the educational gap. The 76% informational gap (best practices, benefits, comparisons, "how to measure…") is where competitors built category authority and Centime is absent. Lowest-difficulty, highest-leverage content to publish first.
  5. Links & PR for a young domain. Head terms need hundreds of referring domains. Earn them via original data/benchmark PR, ERP marketplace + integration-directory listings, and partner co-marketing — not low-quality link buys.
  6. Comparison pages: selective, not central. Keep centime vs X pages for competitors you actually displace; stop expanding the long tail of them.

8. AEO / GEO — earn the AI-engine citations

Why this matters now
AI referrals already convert at ~58% in Centime's CRM, and Centime is measurably present in AI answers today (103 cited pages, 93 mentions; ChatGPT 36, AI Overviews 49, Gemini 12). This is an under-exploited, high-quality channel.
  • Write extractable, statistic-dense, FAQ-structured answers ("What is…", "How does…", "Best… for…"). Adding statistics lifted AI visibility ~+32% and quotations ~+41% (Princeton GEO study).
  • Schema does not substitute for visible answers — AI engines read JSON-LD as plain text. Put the answer in the copy.
  • AI engines barely overlap (~11% shared sources between ChatGPT and Perplexity) — optimize broadly, not for one engine.
  • Get Centime into third-party "top AP automation software" listicles — they rank and are heavily cited by AI engines. Higher leverage than more owned comparison pages.
  • Publish original, anonymized data/benchmarks from Centime's own AP/AR/cash data — uniquely quotable, earns both links and citations.

10.4 — Re-channel internal links (Week 1, ongoing)

You have 859 referring domains but Authority Score 28 — authority that never reaches the money pages. Redirect it internally.

  • ☐ From /features/ap-automation (ranks #1 for brand, your strongest page) → link to /features/ocr, /top-ap-solutions, /top-ap-automation-software-for-netsuite with descriptive anchors.
  • ☐ From the homepage → the ERP-specific converter pages (your proven lead drivers).
  • ☐ From high-traffic meme / finance-humor pages → the relevant AP/AR money page (put that junk traffic to work).
  • ☐ From blog posts (dso-guide, netsuite-roi, cash-flow forecasting posts) → their canonical money page.

10.5 — Technical quick fixes (Week 1)

  • ☐ Resolve www vs non-www duplication. https://centime.com/ar-automation-software ranks separately from the www feature page — enforce one canonical host (301 apex → www) and set canonical tags, or these keep cannibalizing.
  • ☐ Add FAQ/Article schema to every page touched above (visible answers in copy — schema alone doesn't earn AI citations).
  • ☐ Wall off meme keywords from SEO performance reporting so the dashboard reflects pipeline, not vanity traffic.

10.6 — Defend (ongoing)

  • ☐ Monitor /top-ap-automation-software-for-netsuite (p7) and the competitor "vs" pages you already rank #1 for — keep fresh, add schema. Cheap to hold; do not over-invest in new competitor terms.
Expected payoff window
Cannibalization + internal-link fixes (10.1, 10.4–10.5): days–2 weeks. Low-KD enrichment (10.2): 2–6 weeks. New pillar + AEO: 4–8 weeks to first lift. Head terms remain a multi-quarter play.
