What the ground truth revealed

Demand white space (Semrush demand map)
107 high-demand terms we don't own. Invoice/OCR is near-total white space (16.8k mo. searches); AP automation 32k. Absent entirely from invoice automation (2,400), cash flow forecast (1,900), automated invoice processing (1,300).
Visible-but-losing (GSC — high confidence)
Queries where Google already shows Centime at p11–40 with ~0 clicks: the cash-flow cluster (cash flow forecasting software p34, 1,819 impressions) and ERP-specific (ar automation netsuite p13, ap automation for manufacturing p12). Fastest climbs — relevance already established.
AEO is near-zero (1/12) — answers owned by competitors
Perplexity cites Stampli (7×), HighRadius (7×), Tipalti (5×), Zone & Co (4×) — and G2 (3×), so review-platforms + third-party listicles are the lever (data/demand/aeo-listicle-targets.md). Cash-flow + Invoice/OCR are AEO white space too — winnable as first-mover.
